The Kydosist Manifesto And The Automocracy Section I, critique of panic: Traditional existentialism. One might call it a way of life. I call it unnecessary tyranny. Existentialism says you must agonize over meaning, purpose, identity, and many existentialists believe that the universe does not provide any closure as to what they are meant to do in life. This is best explained as the difference between a human, and an object. A pocket-knife is created and forged with purpose. A pocket-knife is meant to cut. Its purpose is pre-determined. But a human’s purpose is unknown. There is no blueprint. You are meant to figure this out through choices. This constructs a profound, agonizing, moral obligation to determine your purpose to fill that void. Unlike existentialism, I believe there is no purpose to life, but with a caveat. I believe that humans are meant to find their purpose in life, based on what they hold dear. This essentially removes the agony of trial and error of existentialism. Section I.I: Kydosism. What is kydosism? Well, kydosism is the belief that your life has no meaning, but you should create your own based on your experiences and what you hold dear to yourself. This removes the unnecessary anguish of desperately searching through your mind to dig out what you think your purpose is. Think of it like this, an existentialist might think, “Well what really makes me a person and is that thing my purpose?”. It puts unnecessary thinking, anguish, on oneself. It puts one into a constant, somewhat perpetual state of overthinking. It transforms the act of simply living into a grueling, hyper-anilitical crisis! It demands a person constantly audit their mind based on every new choice. Even the simplest of choices could dismantle what they believe is their purpose, if it even slightly goes against it. It leaves an individual to rot in a permanent state of stress and overthinking. Even if an existentialist were to make a choice or commit an action against what they believed was their purpose, and not change, then they would become a hypocrite. Existentialism suffers from arrogant idealism. It operates on the false premise that to live a worthy life, one must perpetually endure an agonizing metaphysical mission of search-and-rescue within their own mind. Kydosism provides a grounding clarity, where existentialism doesn't. Kydosism is again the belief that what you hold dear to yourself, is your purpose. Section I.2: The grounding clarity of kydosism is achieved through a selection of a Triad Of Anchors. A kydosist does not get trapped in a loop of endless searching through their morals, to find even a sliver of hope that they had found their meaning. Instead, kydosists focus on what is already tangible. The Triad Of Anchors are those tangible things. The Triad includes Kydos, Oikos, and Kinship. Kydos - Kydos is glory. One's purpose may be determined from their status. This category would include celebrities, politicians, actors, and necessarily anyone who is widely recognizable. But it doesn't always have to include the well known, someone in the pursuit of personal achievement, triumph, or heroism-leaving an undeniable legacy upon the world. Oikos - Oikos is the accumulation of financial autonomy or independence, material stability, comfort, or security of one’s domain. Someone seeking wealth, properties, or material things may fall under this category. Kinship - Kinship is the cultivation of localized emotional bonds-specifically encapsulating friends, family, or the unconditional love of loyal pets. Someone who’s dream is to raise children or start a family, experience good times with friends, or just seem less lonely with pets might fall under this category. Section I.3: But the Triad Of Anchors isn’t the only values one might hold dear. Other pillars inside one’s mind may be creativity, intellectualism, philanthropy, or anything someone might find important to themselves. The Triad is often the most common, that apply to nearly everyone, but people could have their own personal Pillars. An example could be someone's personal Triad Of Anchors: Techne - Techne represents the pursuit of absolute localized purpose through the act of creation itself. One might compose music, write literature or plays, paint, engineer, code, and so much more. Techne is the art of bringing something new of existence into the world, which becomes an ultimate anchor against the lack of purpose. Episteme - Episteme represents the relentless pursuit of truth discovery, and intellectual mastery. The understanding of complex machines, reality, physics, or history. One might hold their own mind dear to them, be able to achieve great things, and that fills their purpose. The knowledge that one has the capacity of achievement. Phronesis - Phronesis represents the moral and altruistic drive to reduce suffering, protect the vulnerable, and engineer systemic reform. People who fall into this category usually focus on improving human conditions around the world, or even in their home city. It doesn't only apply to human conditions, people who could fall under this category also might focus on ecological preservation or animal welfare. To conclude section I, Kydosism completely dismantles the nonsensical panic of traditional thought caused by existentialism by transforming the empty canvas of freedom into something tangible. Whether an individual constructs their reality upon the Original Triad of Kydos, Oikos, and Kinship or engineers their own, entirely personalized Triad of stability, the framework adapts flawlessly, and this is the flaw of existentialism. This exposes the fundamental flaws of nihilism, that there is nothing that truly matters in the grand scheme of things, and though it may be true for thousands upon thousands of years into the future, you won't be there to witness it, but you are here now. Today, and what matters is what you hold dear to yourself. However, this radical liberation does not exist in an impossible psychological vacuum. To claim absolute authority over the purpose of one’s life and construction of their meaning means that a person must also accept absolute accountability for its trajectory. When a chosen pillar falters or falls (for whatever reason)-when wealth is lost or creation fails, there is no cosmic fate or divine intervention to blame. One must blame only themselves and stand face to face with the mental, physical, or philosophical consequences of their actions. This transitions into the mechanics of the Neo-Sartre Hypothesis. Section II The Neo-Sartre Hypothesis: Are our own free actions the direct cause of mental anguish? It depends purely on who you ask. A kydosist would give you a definitive yes. This is the Neo-Sartre Hypothesis. Truly, it's more of a thought rather than a hypothesis, but it asks whether our actions, that we commit with our own human freedom, decide and determine our mental anguish. A kydosist believes that there is no “safety net” from God or any other religious figure, that your actions are your own and yours only, and that any consequences of your actions are caused by those same actions. But asking a determinist would paint a separate picture. Determinists believe free actions do not exist, that everything is predetermined. From the small actions like the movement of a hand, to life-changing choices like choosing a university to attend. But even people who aren't kydosists, or determinists could argue both yes and no. This is shown through the Political Assassin dilemma. A man plans a perfect murder. He shoots a politician in the chest during a speech, but the politician survives and the assassin is arrested. The assassin might mourn or grieve for their old life, or feel regret for throwing their life away for no meaningful change. But one might argue that the inaction of assassinating the same politician could also cause mental anguish as it is the guilt of cowardice. Section II.2: Inaction, is an action itself. The action of not committing an action is an action. The political assassin dilemma exposes a critical psychological loop within the structural framework of kydosism. If mental anguish is the inescapable tax of absolute human agency, then stagnation provides no sanctuary. Traditional existentialism suggests that mental anguish primarily arises after an action is taken; kydosism argues that the inaction itself generates an equally corrosive psychological state: the guilt of cowardice. When an individual consciously reflects upon their subjective pillars, whether they desire the personal glory of Kydos, the financial freedom of Oikos, or the safety of Kinship-the decision to do nothing to actually secure those pillars is a choice, or an action. To idly sit in a meaningless universe while witnessing the systemic decay of what one holds dear is an act of radical submission. The resulting anguish is no one's fault but oneself, and is not caused by an external failure, but one that is rooted deep inside one's body. One has actively chosen to betray their anchors. It is precisely within this psychological friction that underground friction is born. When the burden of one’s inaction becomes more agonizing and painful than the supposed risk of failure, individuals are driven out of isolation. They seek out like-minded agents who refuse to not support their subjective pillars. However, when these individuals band together to reclaim their freedom, their agency collides with the macro-structures of society. To observe this collision in its most extreme form, one must exit the physical boundaries of their individual psychology and enter the unyielding mechanisms of the engineered state. Section III To fully evaluate the ultimate collision between the individual kydosistic agency and systemic oppression, one must analyze past the individual psyche and examine the macro-structural machinery of the state. The geopolitical landscape in question is a unique dystopian anomaly: a sovereign city-state operating under the strict laws of a Nomocratic Automocracy. This form of government was engineered centuries prior by a select transcontinental executive council-composing precisely one legal representative from each recognized continent. This society is bound to an unchanging legal script. Within this jurisdiction, the concept of government has been entirely neutralized and automated; there are no legal representatives, officials, legislative debates, votes, factions, and no living rulers. There is no left-versus-right or red-versus-blue spectrum. The power has been entirely engineered out of human hands, but in the unbending bureaucratic automation of ancient laws that cannot be altered, repealed, replaced, or amended, It is a limbo; no government to protest, yet the state’s laws are ruthlessly enforced by a humanless system.. Section III.2: The terrifying stability of the Automocracy relies on the suppression of individuality. The ancient transcontinental committee recognized that human instinctual passion is the primary threat to an automated legal loop. Therefore, the frozen document targets the three natural pillars of human fulfillment, outlawing them under three decrees: Decree I: The Prohibition of Kydos. To prevent the rise of charismatic figures that could taint the laws and put the public against the laws which had automated them for centuries, all forms of individual triumph and personalized legacy are classified as high treason. Excellence is systemically flattened. Recognition belongs exclusively to the mechanism of the state; any citizen that attempts to raise their name above the baseline population is immediately flagged by automated surveillance as an ideological contagion. Decree II: The Prohibition of Oikos. True financial independence and autonomy creates an economic sanctuary where the state cannot easily penetrate. To dismantle this, the laws outlaw private land ownership, family inheritance, and the accumulation of localized wealth. Citizens are granted fixed monthly stipends managed by automated distribution centers around the state. By freezing the Oikos, the state ensures the survival of the people completely depends on the submission to the automated infrastructure. Decree III: The Prohibition of Kinship. The deepest threat to a faceless bureaucracy is the emotional support provided by family, deep friendships, and companionship from animals. The code strictly and aggressively atomizes the population by treating deep emotional bonds as unauthorized attachments. Domestic spaces are monitored closely to ensure relationships remain sterile and almost transactionary-preventing the formation of the intimate networks that naturally form through human-to-human or human-to-animal connection. Conclusion “You won't be here to witness it, but you are here now. Today, and what matters is what you hold dear to yourself.”
